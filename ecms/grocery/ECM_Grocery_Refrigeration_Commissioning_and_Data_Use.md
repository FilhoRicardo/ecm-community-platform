# ECM: Refrigeration Commissioning and Use of Store Performance Data

## Summary
Commission refrigeration thoroughly and use measured performance from new and existing stores to refine future grocery designs and operating settings.

## Estimated Savings
- **Typical effect**: protects and scales multiple refrigeration ECMs rather than acting as one isolated savings item.
- **End-Uses Affected**: whole refrigeration system and interacting HVAC behavior.

## Basis / References
- The grocery guide includes a refrigeration commissioning guide and explicitly recommends using data from new and existing stores.
- Portfolio learning is central because grocery systems are controls- and operation-sensitive.

## Assumptions
Requires trendable controls and a willingness to compare stores and learn from outliers.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Supports EEVs, compressor staging, heat recovery, humidity control, and prototype refinement.

## Implementation Essentials
- Commission setpoints, defrost, case temperatures, alarms, and heat recovery explicitly.
- Build a recurring review of measured suction/condensing behavior and energy.
- Compare stores by climate and format.
- Feed findings into future prototype and retrofit standards.

## Risks / Constraints
- Refrigeration data often exist but are not normalized or reviewed.
- Poor field service practices can quickly undo design intent.

## KPIs
- Refrigeration kWh by store
- Case temperature compliance
- Alarm closure rate
- Design-vs-operating setpoint drift

## M&V Plan
- Use refrigeration trend data and normalized store comparisons.
- Review outliers and post-commissioning corrections systematically.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low soft-cost measure with very high persistence and portfolio value.
