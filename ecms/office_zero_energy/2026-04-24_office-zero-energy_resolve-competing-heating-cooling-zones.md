# ECM: Resolve Competing Heating/Cooling Zones (Simultaneous H+C)

## Summary

**What:** Identify and eliminate BMS control logic or perimeter/core imbalances causing heating and cooling systems to operate simultaneously in the same zone. Primarily a BMS programming fix with physical investigation first.

**Why:** At 5 Keizers specifically, simultaneous H+C is documented — perimeter heating fighting chilled ceilings in the same zone wastes enormous energy. Root cause is typically control logic errors, poor zone mapping, or incorrect setpoint staging.

## Estimated Savings
- 5–15% HVAC energy
- Building-specific

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: 5 Keizers specifically; likely present at others

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Run energy audit to quantify simultaneous H+C waste — check heating valve % and cooling valve % in same zone simultaneously. 2) Investigate root cause: BMS logic error, comfort complaint driving opposing systems, or physical imbalance. 3) Fix BMS staging logic — ensure heating and cooling cannot call simultaneously in the same zone (set up interlocking). 4) Verify with thermal imaging of zone surfaces. 5) Document the fix and add to seasonal commissioning checklist.

## Risks / Constraints
- HIGH ROI if root cause is BMS logic. Do energy audit to quantify waste before committing to physical works.

## KPIs
- Simultaneous H+C hours (% of operating time)
- Heating valve position (%)
- Cooling valve position (%)
- HVAC energy (kBtu/sq ft/yr)

## M&V Plan (IPMVP Option C with submetering where feasible)
- Pre-installation baseline: 90-day continuous monitoring
- Post-installation: compare same period following year (weather-normalised)
- IPMVP Option C: Whole-building metering with regression analysis

- Payback: 1–6 months
