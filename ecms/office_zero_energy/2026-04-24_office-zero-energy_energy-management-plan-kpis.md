# ECM: Energy Management Plan (EMP) with KPIs and Formal Reporting

## Summary

**What:** Formalise energy management into a documented plan with: energy baselines (normalised for weather and occupancy), KPI targets, seasonal setpoint review schedules, and regular reporting to building management and tenants.

**Why:** Appears at 6 of 8 properties without evidence of implementation. Absence of formal EMPs explains why ECM-01 (setpoint optimisation) is being recommended at 7 buildings — without a plan, no one owns the problem.

## Estimated Savings
- Indirect — multiplies all other ECM savings
- Prevents savings regression; sustains 80–100% of ECM savings over 5 years

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: XYZ, SOM, ITO, One Coleman Street, Radisson Blu Stansted, UN Studio Tower (6/8)

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Appoint named Energy Manager or external consultant with budget and authority. 2) Establish baselines — weather-normalised kWh/sq ft/yr for each utility. 3) Define KPI targets by building and system (kWh/sq ft/yr HVAC, lighting kW, etc.). 4) Create seasonal setpoint review calendar. 5) Monthly reporting to building management. 6) Annual review vs targets — adjust ECM pipeline accordingly.

## Risks / Constraints
- Only as good as the person who owns it. Assign a named Energy Manager with budget and authority to act.

## KPIs
- kWh/sq ft/yr (weather-normalised)
- Energy spend (£/sq ft/yr)
- ECM savings sustained vs baseline (%)
- Review actions completed (%)

## M&V Plan (IPMVP Option C with submetering where feasible)
- Pre-installation baseline: 90-day continuous monitoring
- Post-installation: compare same period following year (weather-normalised)
- IPMVP Option C: Whole-building metering with regression analysis

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Payback: <1 month (staff time only)
- Cost: Requires building-specific survey and contractor pricing
