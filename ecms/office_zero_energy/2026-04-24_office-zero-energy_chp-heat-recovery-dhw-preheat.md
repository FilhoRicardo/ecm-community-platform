# ECM: CHP Heat Recovery (Plate Heat Exchangers for DHW Pre-heating)

## Summary

**What:** Install plate heat exchangers to recover waste heat from CHP exhaust/jacket water cooling circuits to pre-heat domestic hot water, reducing boiler gas load. At Radisson Blu Stansted, CHP is already installed.

**Why:** CHP rejects significant heat continuously via jacket water and exhaust. Heat recovery exchangers can capture 40–60% of this thermal energy and redirect it to DHW preheating, displacing gas boiler consumption.

## Estimated Savings
- 10–20% DHW heating energy
- Building-specific

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: Radisson Blu Stansted

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Measure CHP run-hours and load factor — if <4,000 hrs/year, payback stretches. 2) Verify gas and electricity price differential still makes CHP economic at current rates (shifted since 2021). 3) Size plate HX for CHP heat rejection capacity. 4) Install DHW preheat loop. 5) Commission — verify no backdrafting of combustion appliances. 6) Measure DHW gas consumption before/after.

## Risks / Constraints
- Only applicable where CHP already installed and running >4,000 hrs/yr. Verify current economics — CHP viability has shifted since 2021 energy price changes.

## KPIs
- CHP run-hours (hrs/yr)
- DHW return temperature (°F)
- Natural Gas usage for DHW (therms/month)
- Heat recovered (Btu/hr)
