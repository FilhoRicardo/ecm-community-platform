# ECM: Heat Pump Replacement of Gas Boilers

## Summary

**What:** Replace gas-fired heating plant with air-source or ground-source heat pumps. At 5 Keizers, a single gas supply feeds all five buildings — concentration of risk and opportunity. At Radisson Blu Stansted, electrification aligns with hotel net-zero targets.

**Why:** At current UK/NL energy prices, electrification of heat at COP 3+ is cost-competitive or better with gas, and eliminates Scope 1 carbon. For a portfolio with net-zero targets this is the highest-impact long-term measure.

## Estimated Savings
- Scope 1 elimination; 30–60% heating cost reduction at COP 3+
- Building-specific

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: 5 Keizers, Radisson Blu Stansted
- UK Heat Pump Ready Phase 3 grant
- Dutch ISDE warmtepomp subsidie

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Commission feasibility study and heat loss assessment. 2) Assess distribution system compatibility — low-temperature (45–55°C) vs high-temperature (80°C) required. 3) Size heat pumps for 80% of peak load with gas as backup. 4) Plan electrical infrastructure upgrade (transformer, distribution). 5) Coordinate with DNO for grid connection capacity. 6) 3–5 year capital programme timeline.

## Risks / Constraints
- Strategically essential, operationally complex. Right measure, wrong timescale for 'Low Cost' classification. Plan as 3–5 year capital programme.

## KPIs
- Gas consumption (therms/yr)
- Scope 1 emissions (tCO2e/yr)
- Heat pump COP
- Heating cost (£/yr)

## M&V Plan (IPMVP Option C with submetering where feasible)
- Pre-installation baseline: 90-day continuous monitoring
- Post-installation: compare same period following year (weather-normalised)
- IPMVP Option C: Whole-building metering with regression analysis

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Payback: 5–15 years (without carbon incentives); 3–7 years with current UK/NL grants
- Cost: Requires building-specific survey and contractor pricing
