# ECM: Building Envelope Insulation (BOH / Exposed Zones)

## Summary

**What:** Improve insulation in back-of-house areas adjacent to uninsulated exterior zones — cold bridging and unenclosed heated areas driving heat loss. Primarily applicable to Radisson Blu Stansted where exposed zones were identified.

**Why:** Back-of-house areas adjacent to uninsulated exterior zones drive disproportionate heat loss. Thermal imaging at Radisson identified specific cold-bridging issues in BOH zones adjacent to the exterior.

## Estimated Savings
- 3–10% heating energy
- Building-specific

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: Radisson Blu Stansted specifically

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Commission thermal imaging survey to quantify envelope deficiencies. 2) Identify high-priority zones (highest heat loss per £ of insulation cost). 3) Specify insulation solution (internal dry-lining, external cladding, orUU pipe insulation for exposed pipes). 4. Obtain planning/conservation approval if exterior work is needed. 5) Commission post-installation thermal imaging to verify improvement.

## Risks / Constraints
- Savings highly building-specific and often modest in well-insulated commercial buildings. Requires thermal imaging survey to quantify before committing.

## KPIs
- Heat loss (W/m²)
- Heating energy (kBtu/sq ft/yr)
- Thermal imaging delta (°C improvement)

## M&V Plan (IPMVP Option C with submetering where feasible)
- Pre-installation baseline: 90-day continuous monitoring
- Post-installation: compare same period following year (weather-normalised)
- IPMVP Option C: Whole-building metering with regression analysis

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Payback: 5–15 years
- Cost: Requires building-specific survey and contractor pricing
