# ECM: Full LED Luminaire Upgrade

## Summary

**What:** Full luminaire replacement with LED, including landlord and tenant areas. At One Coleman Street, ground floor is done — extend to remaining floors phased with refurbishment. 50–60% reduction vs T5 fluorescent; 70–80% vs older T8/halogen.

**Why:** In commercial buildings where lighting is 20–30% of electricity, LED upgrade delivers 10–18% of total electricity bill reduction. Combined with controls (ECM-18), savings reach 70–80% of original lighting energy.

## Estimated Savings
- 50–70% reduction in lighting energy vs T8/halogen; 10–18% of total electricity bill
- 0.4–0.8 W/sq ft reduction

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: XYZ Building, One Coleman Street (partial), UN Studio Tower
- CIBSE SLL Lighting Guide

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Survey all luminaires — note type, length, ballast, condition. 2) Phase by refurbishment events (lease-end works, planned maintenance). 3) Specify LED with appropriate colour temperature (4000K for offices) and dimming capability. 4) Co-specify ECM-18 controls — dimming + occupancy. 5) Verify egress illuminance post-retrofit. 6) Tenant area replacements require lease clause cooperation or lease event.

## Risks / Constraints
- Don't spec LED-only without also specifying controls (ECM-18) — dimming and occupancy sensing add 20–30% additional saving.

## KPIs
- Lighting power density (W/sq ft)
- Lighting kWh/month
- Tenant area vs landlord area split
