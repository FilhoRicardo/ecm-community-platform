# ECM: Zero-Energy Commissioning and Early-Operation Verification

## Summary
Use commissioning and early-operation verification to ensure low-energy office strategies and renewable systems actually perform as intended after occupancy.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: persistence and realization measure rather than a single end-use retrofit.
- **Typical effect**: prevents drift and protects the value of all other ECMs.
- **End-Uses Affected**: all major energy systems indirectly.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office, Chapters 2 and 3 emphasize maintaining zero-energy goals through commissioning, early operation, and long-term tracking.
- The guide explicitly includes commissioning, OPR/BOD alignment, and early-operation verification as part of the process.

## Assumptions
Requires active owner participation, issue tracking, and trend review rather than ceremonial handover-only commissioning.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Improves persistence of every major ECM—envelope, lighting, plug loads, HVAC, and PV.

## Implementation Essentials
- Commission controls sequences, ventilation delivery, lighting controls, and renewable systems with trend-based verification.
- Compare actual operation with OPR/BOD during the first full seasonal cycle.
- Retune schedules, sensors, deadbands, and diagnostics before operational drift becomes normalized.
- Keep a live issues log through early occupancy.

## Risks / Constraints
- Savings decay rapidly if commissioning stops at substantial completion.
- FM teams may inherit sophisticated systems without sufficient training.

## KPIs
- Issue log closure rate
- Modeled versus actual EUI in early operation
- Control override frequency
- PV and major-system runtime anomalies

## M&V Plan
- Whole-building Option C supported by system-level trends and meters.
- Use seasonal trend reviews rather than one-time spot checks.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Soft-cost ECM with usually strong lifecycle value because it protects multiple capital measures at once.
