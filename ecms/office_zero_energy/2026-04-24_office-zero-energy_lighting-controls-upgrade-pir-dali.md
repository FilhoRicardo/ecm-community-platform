# ECM: Lighting Controls Upgrade (PIR / Occupancy / DALI Dimming)

## Summary

**What:** Add passive infrared occupancy sensors and daylight-responsive DALI dimming to lighting circuits, particularly in corridors, risers, and intermittently occupied spaces. At XYZ Building, risers are confirmed lit 24/7 with no control.

**Why:** Corridors, risers, and meeting rooms are occupied rarely but lit continuously. PIR occupancy sensors and daylight dimming in perimeter zones dramatically reduce lighting kWh without impacting occupant comfort.

## Estimated Savings
- 20–40% lighting energy in controlled zones
- 0.3–0.8 W/sq ft

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: all 8 properties
- IES Lighting Handbook 10th Ed.
- CIBSE LL6

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Audit lighting circuits — identify always-on circuits (risers, corridors, stairwells). 2) Specify DALI-compatible drivers for dimming zones
2. PIR sensors for switching zones. 3. Install sensors — stairwells and corridors need wide-angle PIR
3. offices need ceiling-mounted sensors. 4) Commission: set time delays (15 min for occupancy, continuous for daylight dimming). 5) Verify egress illuminance is maintained at all times.

## Risks / Constraints
- DALI retrofit requires DALI-compatible drivers — check existing luminaire compatibility before specifying.

## KPIs
- Lighting kW by zone
- Sensor activation rate (%)
- Lighting kWh/month

## M&V Plan (IPMVP Option C with submetering where feasible)
- Pre-installation baseline: 90-day continuous monitoring
- Post-installation: compare same period following year (weather-normalised)
- IPMVP Option C: Whole-building metering with regression analysis
