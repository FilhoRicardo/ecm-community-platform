# ECM: Rooftop Solar PV

## Summary

**What:** Rooftop PV array sized to offset landlord common area or whole-building electricity consumption. At Radisson Blu Stansted, up to 40% bill reduction identified. At Akzo Nobel, 26–28 kWp already exists.

**Why:** One of the highest-impact measures in the portfolio, especially at the hotel where load profile (daytime, high DHW) aligns well with solar generation. Rooftop space at Radisson and other properties has been identified as viable.

## Estimated Savings
- 10–40% of landlord common area or whole-building electricity
- Building-specific; Radisson up to 40% identified

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: XYZ, Radisson Blu Stansted (40% identified), SOM, ITO, One Coleman Street, Akzo Nobel (26–28 kWp existing)
- MCS Installation Standard
- UK SEG (Smart Export Guarantee)

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Solar feasibility study — roof load-bearing capacity, planning consent, grid connection capacity and DNO export limits, shading analysis. 2) Design system for self-consumption ratio vs export — if export only, economics require PPA. 3) Specify panel type (monocrystalline for efficiency), inverter sizing. 4) Battery storage only if DNO export is curtailed. 5) Commission and register with MCS.

## Risks / Constraints
- The 40% figure at Radisson needs scrutiny — only achievable if building load profile aligns well with generation. Verify with half-hourly load vs generation overlap analysis before sizing.

## KPIs
- PV generation (kWh/yr)
- Self-consumption rate (%)
- Grid export (kWh/yr)
- Bill reduction (£/yr)
