# ECM: Luminaire-Level Lighting Control (LLLC)

## Summary
Use luminaire-level digital lighting control to improve zoning granularity for daylight response, scheduling, and occupancy-based adjustment in office spaces.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: strategy-driven control improvement rather than a single fixed savings claim.
- **Typical effect**: higher realized control savings and easier recommissioning compared with coarse legacy zoning.
- **End-Uses Affected**: lighting electricity and control persistence.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office includes a dedicated LLLC sidebar and broader control strategy sections.
- This matches the benchmark preference for controls that are both actionable and trendable.

## Assumptions
Most suitable in projects already planning LED lighting and digital controls.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Enhances daylight harvesting, scheduling, and occupancy response.

## Implementation Essentials
- Select an interoperable digital control platform with robust trend and commissioning capability.
- Map control groups to real daylight and occupancy conditions.
- Use LLLC where flexibility or reconfiguration is expected over time.
- Train FM staff on diagnostics, overrides, and trend review.

## Risks / Constraints
- Overcomplicated commissioning can delay benefits if not resourced properly.
- Vendor lock-in or poor integration can increase lifecycle risk.
- User interfaces must remain understandable.

## KPIs
- Lighting kWh by zone/group
- Dimming fraction
- Override counts
- Reconfiguration effort after tenant changes

## M&V Plan
- Use control trend data as the primary M&V source.
- Compare daytime and after-hours runtime against design expectations after stabilization.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Moderate premium over simpler controls; strongest value in flexible high-performance fit-outs.
