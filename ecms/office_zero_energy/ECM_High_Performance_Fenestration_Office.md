# ECM: High-Performance Fenestration with Balanced U-Factor, SHGC, and VT

## Summary
Use whole-assembly fenestration performance, not center-of-glass values, to balance daylight quality, solar control, and thermal comfort in office façades.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: project-specific energy impact through façade heat transfer and daylight quality.
- **Typical effect**: significant where WWR is moderate to high or where daylight strategy depends on useful visible transmittance.
- **End-Uses Affected**: heating, cooling, lighting, and perimeter comfort.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office, Chapter 5 Envelope includes thermal performance of fenestration and doors.
- Chapter 5 Daylighting reinforces balancing heat transfer with daylight performance.

## Assumptions
Assumes façade design still allows product selection and orientation-specific tuning.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones. Low U-factor matters more in colder climates; SHGC control matters more in hot and mixed climates.

## Interaction Notes
Strong interaction with daylighting, glare control, external shading, and HVAC sizing.

## Implementation Essentials
- Specify whole-window U-factor, SHGC, and VT by façade type and orientation where practical.
- Coordinate frame choice and thermal breaks with assembly-level targets.
- Review glazing color, reflectance, and visual quality with architectural requirements.
- Avoid solving solar gain by simply darkening the façade excessively.

## Risks / Constraints
- Center-of-glass assumptions can misstate actual performance.
- Design teams may over-prioritize aesthetics and under-prioritize useful VT.
- Poorly integrated shading or blinds can negate daylight intent.

## KPIs
- Whole-assembly U-factor/SHGC/VT
- Perimeter discomfort complaints
- Perimeter lighting kWh
- Cooling load attributable to glazing

## M&V Plan
- Use façade-performance review during design and procurement.
- Post-occupancy, verify with lighting/HVAC trends and occupant comfort feedback in perimeter zones.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Moderate façade premium in many projects; often justified by HVAC and comfort benefits if coordinated early.
