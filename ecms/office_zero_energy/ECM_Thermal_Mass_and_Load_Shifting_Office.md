# ECM: Thermal Mass and Load Shifting

## Summary
Use exposed or integrated thermal mass to moderate temperature swings and support load shifting in low-load office designs where daily cycling patterns justify it.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: strategy support measure rather than a uniform prescriptive savings item.
- **Typical effect**: strongest where diurnal swing, control strategy, and occupancy patterns align.
- **End-Uses Affected**: peak cooling, peak heating, and load timing.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office includes Thermal Mass and a sidebar on Phase-Change Materials.
- Chapter 4 also treats form, shape, and load behavior as major simulation variables.

## Assumptions
Benefits depend heavily on climate, control, ventilation/night-flush potential, and whether mass is actually exposed to occupied air.

## Climate Zone Relevance
Most useful in climates with meaningful diurnal temperature swing or load-shifting value.

## Interaction Notes
Works with shading, low internal gains, and controlled night ventilation strategies.

## Implementation Essentials
- Preserve exposed slab or interior mass where architectural program allows.
- Do not assume structural mass is beneficial if finishes isolate it from the space.
- Model pre-cooling, night flush, or control resets before crediting savings.
- Use this as a system-integration strategy, not a substitute for poor envelope or controls.

## Risks / Constraints
- Benefits are easy to overstate if not modeled.
- Tenant fit-outs can cover exposed mass later.
- Night-flush strategies may be incompatible with outdoor air quality, acoustics, or security.

## KPIs
- Peak demand reduction
- Nighttime precooling or flush hours
- Indoor temperature swing
- Modeled versus actual load-shifting response

## M&V Plan
- Use simulation as the primary design-stage verification.
- Post-occupancy, review peak demand profile and overnight temperature/load behavior.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Usually low if integrated with base building architecture; can be costly if pursued late.
