# ECM: Plug Load Inventory and Right-Sizing

## Summary
Perform a plug load inventory and correct inflated design assumptions so the office is not overdesigned electrically or thermally.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: plug loads are one of the top three office end uses across climates and should be managed early.
- **Guide reference**: a thorough inventory plus reduction strategy can reduce plug loads by roughly 20–50% in many office contexts.
- **End-Uses Affected**: plug electricity directly, plus cooling and renewable-system sizing indirectly.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office, Chapter 5 notes that requested office plug loads are often far above measured reality.
- The guide explicitly recommends performing a plug load inventory and reduction strategy.

## Assumptions
Requires actual device counts, diversity assumptions, and occupant/tenant engagement.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Reduces both direct electricity use and cooling/system sizing burden. Strongly affects PV sizing in zero-energy projects.

## Implementation Essentials
- Inventory work-area, break-room, and building process loads separately.
- Validate actual demand rather than accepting leasing-stage request values at face value.
- Use the inventory to set design criteria, procurement rules, and control opportunities.
- Update cooling-load and electrical-distribution assumptions after inventory results are known.

## Risks / Constraints
- Tenant negotiations may still push for inflated allowances.
- Unmanaged future fit-outs can reintroduce excessive plug loads.
- IT/security/process loads must be classified accurately.

## KPIs
- Plug load W/ft² by area type
- After-hours base electrical load
- Peak plug demand versus design allowance
- Plug-load share of whole-building EUI

## M&V Plan
- Use branch/panel metering where feasible or whole-building interval analysis with nighttime baseload review.
- Track actual plug intensity against the design inventory after occupancy.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low-capex analytical ECM with high downstream value for system sizing and policy setting.
