# ECM: High-Efficiency Transformers and Power Distribution Optimization

## Summary
Reduce electrical-distribution losses through efficient transformers, realistic demand assumptions, and minimized unnecessary conversion stages.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: incremental but important reduction in distribution losses, especially in very low-EUI offices.
- **Typical effect**: smaller than HVAC or lighting ECMs, but increasingly relevant as whole-building EUI falls.
- **End-Uses Affected**: transformer/distribution losses and system efficiency.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office includes Power Distribution Systems and notes that transformer losses should be included in energy targets.
- The guide also warns that conservative demand sizing may oversize transformers relative to actual demand.

## Assumptions
Best in new construction, major electrical upgrades, or offices with unusual conversion redundancy.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Complements plug-load inventory because realistic demand assumptions support right-sized electrical infrastructure.

## Implementation Essentials
- Review realistic load diversity and measured demand instead of worst-case tenant requests.
- Specify high-efficiency transformers appropriate to actual loading range.
- Minimize unnecessary conversion steps in distribution architecture.
- Coordinate with emergency and critical-power strategies where relevant.

## Risks / Constraints
- Conservative design practices can lock in oversized equipment.
- Benefits may be ignored because losses are less visible than end-use savings.

## KPIs
- Transformer no-load and load losses
- Measured transformer loading ratio
- Distribution loss estimate as % of whole-building electricity
- Peak demand versus design allowance

## M&V Plan
- Use design calculations plus metered loading after occupancy.
- Compare transformer loading against expected efficiency range.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Incremental in new projects; weaker standalone retrofit economics unless replacement is already planned.
