# ECM: ML/AI HVAC Optimisation Overlay

## Summary

**What:** Third-party AI/ML platform (e.g. Noda, Verdigris, BrainBox AI) that sits above the BMS, uses weather forecasts, occupancy data, and historical patterns to pre-condition buildings and auto-tune setpoints.

**Why:** Appears at all 8 properties — but only after BMS fundamentals are correct. If setpoints and schedules are wrong, AI will optimise around broken inputs. Listed everywhere suggests reflexive recommendation rather than sequenced approach.

## Estimated Savings
- 8–12% HVAC energy (real-world); vendor claims 15–25%
- Building-specific

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: all 8 properties

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Fix ECM-01 (setpoints) and ECM-02 (trend logging) first — baseline must be valid. 2) Shortlist 2–3 platforms — request pilot proposal with performance guarantee. 3) Validate vendor claims against building-specific data (not generic case studies). 4) Negotiate contract: data ownership, performance guarantee, exit clauses. 5) Commission platform with BMS integration — verify AI inputs are clean. 6) Monthly review of actual vs projected savings.

## Risks / Constraints
- ⚠️ Step 4, not step 1. Fix BMS fundamentals first. Vendor claims of 15–25% require good baseline data and active commissioning — real-world often 8–12%.

## KPIs
- HVAC kBtu/sq ft/yr vs baseline
- AI setpoint recommendations accepted (%)
- Savings vs contract guarantee (%)

## M&V Plan (IPMVP Option C with submetering where feasible)
- Pre-installation baseline: 90-day continuous monitoring
- Post-installation: compare same period following year (weather-normalised)
- IPMVP Option C: Whole-building metering with regression analysis

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Payback: 12–24 months
- Cost: Requires building-specific survey and contractor pricing
