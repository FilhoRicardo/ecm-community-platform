# ECM: Orientation-Led Glazing Allocation for Office Buildings

## Summary
Prioritize north/south-oriented glazing and limit uncontrolled east/west glazing so daylight is improved without creating unnecessary cooling, glare, or perimeter comfort penalties.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: project-specific whole-building impact through lower solar gains, better daylighting, and reduced peak loads.
- **Typical effect**: strongest as a load-reduction enabler rather than a stand-alone savings item.
- **End-Uses Affected**: perimeter cooling, daylight-linked lighting energy, and sometimes heating.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office, Chapter 4 identifies orientation, window-to-wall ratio, and shading as major early-phase drivers.
- Chapter 5 Building and Site Planning emphasizes building orientation and daylight access as foundational design choices.

## Assumptions
Applicable where façade design is still flexible. Savings depend on climate, façade exposure, glazing properties, and whether daylight-responsive controls are implemented.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones. Highest value in hot and mixed climates for cooling reduction, but also relevant in cold climates for daylight quality and annual balance.

## Interaction Notes
Strongly interacts with shading devices, SHGC selection, daylight harvesting, and perimeter comfort strategies.

## Implementation Essentials
- Test early massing/orientation options during concept and schematic design.
- Bias the highest-value daylight façades toward north/south exposure where site conditions allow.
- Use lower glazing fractions or stronger solar control on east/west façades.
- Coordinate orientation with workstation layout and tenant planning flexibility.

## Risks / Constraints
- Site constraints may limit orientation freedom.
- Overglazed branding façades can undermine the strategy.
- Poor downstream coordination can erase gains.

## KPIs
- Window area by façade orientation
- Perimeter daylight autonomy or sDA
- Peak cooling load by façade
- Annual lighting and cooling EUI in perimeter zones

## M&V Plan
- Use daylight and whole-building simulation during design to compare options.
- Post-occupancy, verify with interval lighting/HVAC trends in perimeter zones where available.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Primarily a planning ECM. Value is often indirect through reduced HVAC sizing and better daylight performance.
