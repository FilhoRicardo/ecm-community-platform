# ECM: PV-Ready Design and Roof Reservation

## Summary
Reserve unshaded roof area, structure, and electrical pathways so on-site PV can be installed at meaningful scale without redesign conflict.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: enabling measure rather than direct efficiency reduction.
- **Typical effect**: reduces future cost and design friction for the renewable system needed to close the zero-energy gap.
- **End-Uses Affected**: none directly; reduces future renewable implementation risk.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office, Chapter 5 Building and Site Planning includes Planning for Renewable Energy.

## Assumptions
Applies at new construction or major roof/system redesign stages.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones; value depends on solar resource and available unshaded roof area.

## Interaction Notes
Strongly interacts with roof-mounted equipment planning, skylight/toplighting decisions, and structural design.

## Implementation Essentials
- Reserve a defined PV zone free of excessive mechanical congestion and shade risk.
- Protect roof loading capacity, electrical room space, and conduit pathways.
- Coordinate daylighting apertures, penthouses, and maintenance walkways so PV is not stranded to leftover fragments.

## Risks / Constraints
- Late rooftop equipment additions can destroy PV potential.
- Architectural priorities may preserve PV in principle but not in usable area.

## KPIs
- Reserved unshaded roof area
- Estimated PV capacity potential (kW)
- Number of major roof conflicts avoided
- Structural reserve for PV loading

## M&V Plan
- Design-stage verification through roof plan and capacity study.
- Use periodic design reviews to ensure reserved areas remain protected.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low incremental cost if planned early; high lost-opportunity cost if ignored.
