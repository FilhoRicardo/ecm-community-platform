# ECM: High-Performance Opaque Envelope Assemblies

## Summary
Improve roof and wall thermal performance so the office reaches lower annual loads and reduced peak HVAC demand before system selection is finalized.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: project-specific whole-building savings through reduced conductive gains and losses.
- **Typical effect**: most material when coupled with airtightness and reduced glazing heat transfer.
- **End-Uses Affected**: space heating, cooling, and peak HVAC sizing.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office, Chapter 5 Envelope covers thermal performance of opaque assemblies.
- Appendix A provides envelope thermal performance factors to support assembly comparison.

## Assumptions
Most effective in new construction or deep façade/roof retrofit. Savings vary with climate, internal loads, and façade ratio.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones. Greatest relative impact in heating-dominant and mixed climates, but still useful in hot climates for cooling and peak demand reduction.

## Interaction Notes
Pairs with air leakage control, thermal bridging control, and glazing selection.

## Implementation Essentials
- Set target effective R-values by assembly, not nominal insulation only.
- Coordinate continuity at parapets, slab edges, fenestration interfaces, and roof-wall transitions.
- Review condensation and durability implications for the selected climate zone.
- Re-run HVAC loads once the enclosure is materially improved.

## Risks / Constraints
- Value engineering often strips continuity details.
- Thermal performance can be degraded by attachment systems and poor sequencing.
- Retrofits may face geometry and moisture constraints.

## KPIs
- Roof and wall assembly U-values
- Modeled heating and cooling loads
- Peak heating and cooling capacity
- Envelope QA closure rate

## M&V Plan
- Use design-model comparison against baseline envelope assumptions.
- Verify as-built insulation and continuity through submittal review and site inspection.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Often moderate first-cost impact with better lifecycle value when integrated early.
