# ECM: On-Site Photovoltaic System Installation

## Summary
Install on-site PV to offset the remaining office electrical demand after aggressive efficiency and load-reduction measures are in place.

## Estimated Savings
- **ASHRAE AEDG basis**: required final-leg strategy in many zero-energy office pathways rather than an efficiency ECM in the narrow sense.
- **Typical effect**: site-specific offset of purchased electricity based on available area, solar resource, and self-consumption profile.
- **End-Uses Affected**: grid electricity purchased by the building.

## Basis / References
- AEDGZE Small to Medium Office, Chapter 5 Renewable Energy covers design and implementation strategies for PV.
- The guide defines zero energy as producing as much on-site renewable energy as the building consumes annually.

## Assumptions
Requires that efficiency measures are already optimized; otherwise roof area may be insufficient to reach zero energy.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones; yield and economics vary by solar resource and local rules.

## Interaction Notes
Strongly dependent on plug-load management and HVAC/lighting efficiency because these determine the size of renewable system required.

## Implementation Essentials
- Complete roof, structure, shading, and electrical interconnection assessment.
- Model annual PV production against site EUI and load profile.
- Coordinate array layout with roof drainage, maintenance access, and future rooftop equipment risk.
- Trend production and self-consumption after commissioning.

## Risks / Constraints
- Roof obstructions and shading are common underappreciated constraints.
- Interconnection and tariff assumptions can materially affect value.
- Poor maintenance planning can degrade lifecycle output.

## KPIs
- Annual PV kWh
- Self-consumption ratio
- Site electricity offset fraction
- PV uptime / inverter availability

## M&V Plan
- Use direct generation metering and inverter trend data.
- Compare actual annual output against modeled PV yield after weather normalization where appropriate.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Project-specific strategic measure; typically essential for on-site zero energy even when pure payback is not shortest.
