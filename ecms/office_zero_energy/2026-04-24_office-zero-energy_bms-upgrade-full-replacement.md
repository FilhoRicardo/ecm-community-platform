# ECM: BMS Upgrade / Full Replacement

## Summary

**What:** Replace end-of-life or absent BMS with a modern open-protocol (BACnet/MSTP or IP) system with full trend logging, remote access, and integration capability. At 5 Keizers, no dedicated BMS exists.

**Why:** Legacy BMS (SOM Building has 2004 original) limits what other ECMs can achieve. Modern BMS is prerequisite for AI overlay, DCV, occupancy integration, and automated metering.

## Estimated Savings
- Variable (enabler of savings)
- Building-specific

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: SOM Building (2004), ITO Building, 5 Keizers (no BMS)

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Commission energy audit to assess current BMS state. 2) Define MVP scope: trend logging, remote access, BACnet integration, EMS link. 3) Joint procurement with ITO/SOM (shared maintenance team) to reduce cost. 4) Plan 18–24 month timeline: procurement → installation → commissioning. 5) Define handover documentation and operator training.

## Risks / Constraints
- High disruption risk during switchover. Define MVP tightly — scope creep is common.

## KPIs
- BMS points controllable remotely (#)
- Trend data availability (hrs/day)
- HVAC fault resolution time

## M&V Plan (IPMVP Option C with submetering where feasible)
- Pre-installation baseline: 90-day continuous monitoring
- Post-installation: compare same period following year (weather-normalised)
- IPMVP Option C: Whole-building metering with regression analysis

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Payback: 12–36 months
- Cost: Requires building-specific survey and contractor pricing
