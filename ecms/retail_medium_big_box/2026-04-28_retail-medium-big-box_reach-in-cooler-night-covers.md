# ECM: Reach-in Cooler/ Freezer Night Covers

## Summary

Install vinyl night covers on reach-in cooler and freezer cases to reduce heat ingress from store air into refrigerated cases during store closed hours when there is no product access benefit. Refrigerated cases are designed to cool products during customer access hours. At night, cases still run at full capacity to overcome heat infiltration from warm store air. Night covers reduce this infiltration by 30–50%, reducing compressor and fan energy.

## Estimated Savings
- 10–25% refrigeration energy
- 5–15% total store electricity

## Basis / References
- DOE Commercial Refrigeration Guide
- NREL Case Cover Field Study 2016

## Assumptions
- Store operates with open-front cooler/freezer cases
- Night covers can be installed/removed without permanent modification to cases
- Store has closed hours (typically 8+ hours) where no customer access occurs
- Case refrigeration systems are operational and adequately charged

## Implementation Essentials
1. Survey all open-front cooler and freezer cases — document dimensions, type, and door count
2. Order custom vinyl covers sized to each case opening (suppliers: Kason, Component Hardware, or case OEM)
3. Train night staff on proper installation and removal procedure — covers must be in place before store close
4. Verify case temperatures maintained after cover installation using existing case thermometers or data logger
5. Establish a nightly compliance checklist to ensure covers are installed consistently
6. After 30 days, review refrigeration energy submeter data to quantify savings vs. baseline

## Risks / Constraints
- Food safety: verify with health inspector that covers do not compromise required case temperatures or create condensation issues
- Operational: covers must not interfere with emergency lighting, fire suppression, or security systems
- Case integrity: improper fit can cause cover to contact evaporator fans or heating elements — specify correct material (food-grade vinyl, 1/8"–1/4" thickness)
- Condensation management in humid climates — some vinyl covers include vent ports to mitigate moisture buildup

## KPIs
- Refrigeration kW (metered)
- Case temperature variance (°F)
- Compressor runtime hours

## M&V Plan (IPMVP Option C with submetering where feasible)
- IPMVP Option C: Whole-store electricity metering with regression baseline (normalize for ambient temperature and store hours)
- Submetering: dedicated refrigeration circuit meter on each cooler/freezer case bank
- Measurement period: minimum 30 days pre- and post-implementation, same season
- Verify with walk-in cooler/freezer solid covers already in place to isolate incremental impact

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Custom vinyl covers: $50–$150 per opening depending on size
- Typical medium supermarket: 20–60 cooler/freezer openings
- Total material cost: $1,000–$9,000
- Installation labor: $500–$2,000 (one-time)
- Payback: 4–12 months
