# ECM: Lodging Domestic Hot Water Plant Optimization

## Summary
Optimize central domestic hot water generation, storage, recirculation, and setpoint management to reduce one of the most persistent end uses in highway lodging.

## Estimated Savings
- **AEDG basis**: service water heating is a core recommendation category.
- **Typical effect**: meaningful whole-building impact in hotels with steady DHW demand.
- **End-Uses Affected**: DHW fuel/electricity and recirculation losses.

## Basis / References
- The lodging AEDG includes service water heating as a principal technical topic.
- The guide focuses on practical measures suited to small lodging properties rather than complex central-plant architecture.

## Assumptions
Applies to hotels with central DHW serving guestrooms and support spaces. Savings depend on baseline temperature, recirculation practice, and heater efficiency.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Pairs with low-flow fixtures, laundry strategy, and heat-recovery opportunities.

## Implementation Essentials
- Right-size storage and recovery to actual demand profile.
- Reduce excessive recirculation temperature loss and unnecessary pump runtime.
- Insulate piping and eliminate dead-leg distribution where possible.
- Review overnight setback only within health/safety constraints.

## Risks / Constraints
- Legionella and guest-service requirements limit temperature reductions.
- Long piping runs can make distribution losses stubborn.

## KPIs
- DHW fuel or kWh
- Recirculation pump runtime
- Hot-water delivery complaints
- Delivered DHW energy per occupied room

## M&V Plan
- Compare DHW plant energy before/after optimization.
- Track return temperature, pump runtime, and complaint rate.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low to moderate depending on whether changes are control-based or include plant replacement.
