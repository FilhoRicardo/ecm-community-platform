# ECM: Envelope Air Sealing and Glazing Improvement for Guest Comfort

## Summary
Improve air sealing, door weatherstripping, and window/glazing performance to reduce infiltration, perimeter discomfort, and HVAC waste in guestrooms and public areas.

## Estimated Savings
- **Typical effect**: meaningful heating/cooling savings and comfort improvement, especially in older motels and roadside lodging.
- **End-Uses Affected**: heating, cooling, and guest comfort.

## Basis / References
- The lodging AEDG includes envelope recommendations for roofs, walls, doors, and glazing by climate zone.
- Early load reduction is a repeated theme in the guide’s integrated design chapter.

## Assumptions
Best where baseline infiltration is high or windows/doors are aging.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones; strongest in cold, windy, and hot-humid locations.

## Interaction Notes
Supports guestroom HVAC upgrades and occupancy-based setbacks by reducing load volatility.

## Implementation Essentials
- Audit window seals, door sweeps, sleeve penetrations, and façade leakage points.
- Upgrade glazing where replacement cycles justify it.
- Focus on guestrooms first, then lobby/corridor/public areas.
- Combine with room-refresh or façade-maintenance work where possible.

## Risks / Constraints
- Guest disruption must be managed carefully.
- One-for-one replacements may preserve weak detailing if sleeves and frames are ignored.

## KPIs
- Perimeter comfort complaints
- Heating and cooling use per occupied room
- Identified leakage defects resolved
- Infrared or enclosure QA findings

## M&V Plan
- Compare room-block energy and complaint rates before/after work.
- Use spot envelope QA and seasonal trend review.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Variable; strongest when combined with planned maintenance or renovation.
