# ECM: High-Efficiency Guestroom Unitary HVAC Upgrade

## Summary
Replace legacy guestroom PTAC/PTHP or similar unitary equipment with higher-efficiency guestroom HVAC that improves part-load operation, lowers noise, and reduces simultaneous heating/cooling waste.

## Estimated Savings
- **AEDG basis**: HVAC package measure rather than a single fixed savings claim.
- **Typical effect**: meaningful guestroom heating/cooling reduction where legacy through-wall units are inefficient or poorly controlled.
- **End-Uses Affected**: guestroom heating, cooling, and fan energy.

## Basis / References
- The highway lodging AEDG is built around small hotels and motels using unitary HVAC equipment.
- HVAC efficiency and control are core recommendation areas in the climate-zone guidance and implementation chapters.

## Assumptions
Best in buildings with many individually conditioned guestrooms and aging packaged equipment. Savings depend on baseline efficiency, infiltration, and control strategy.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones. Highest heating benefit in cold/mixed climates and cooling benefit in hot climates.

## Interaction Notes
Works best with guestroom occupancy control, envelope air sealing, and lower corridor/ventilation loads.

## Implementation Essentials
- Audit installed equipment type, age, and condition by room block.
- Select replacement units with better part-load efficiency and lower standby fan energy.
- Revisit sleeve, louver, condensate, and acoustic details during replacement.
- Standardize controls and lock out extreme guest setpoints where policy allows.

## Risks / Constraints
- Guestroom access and outage planning are operationally sensitive.
- Poor sleeve/louver conditions can undermine expected performance.
- Lowest-cost one-for-one replacement may preserve old control problems.

## KPIs
- HVAC kWh/room-night
- Heating fuel per occupied room
- Guest comfort complaints
- Average guestroom runtime by season

## M&V Plan
- Compare room-block HVAC energy and complaint rate before/after replacement.
- Normalize by occupancy and weather.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Moderate capex; strongest when timed with room refresh or replacement cycle.
