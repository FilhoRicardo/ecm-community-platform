# ECM: Lodging Commissioning, Scheduling, and Operational Persistence

## Summary
Use commissioning, scheduling discipline, and routine operational review to preserve savings from guestroom HVAC, lighting, DHW, and ventilation measures.

## Estimated Savings
- **Typical effect**: protects the value of multiple ECMs rather than acting as a stand-alone hardware savings measure.
- **End-Uses Affected**: whole-building operations.

## Basis / References
- The lodging AEDG explicitly emphasizes QA, commissioning, and operation through the building life cycle.
- The guide notes that early decisions only produce savings if implemented and maintained correctly.

## Assumptions
Requires owner/operator engagement and periodic review of controls and complaints.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Supports every other ECM in the lodging package.

## Implementation Essentials
- Trend guestroom unit schedules, setback logic, exterior lighting schedules, and DHW operation.
- Review complaint hot spots rather than allowing permanent overrides.
- Recommission after major room refresh or PMS/control changes.
- Benchmark energy per occupied room monthly.

## Risks / Constraints
- Staff turnover can erode control discipline.
- Permanent overrides can quietly remove savings over time.

## KPIs
- Energy per occupied room-night
- Override counts
- Complaint-to-resolution cycle time
- Monthly utility benchmark drift

## M&V Plan
- Use whole-building Option C with occupancy normalization.
- Supplement with targeted trend review of guestroom HVAC and lighting runtime.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low soft-cost measure with strong persistence value.


## Templates / Reuse
- ECM screening worksheet
- Climate-zone applicability note
- KPI / M&V checklist
