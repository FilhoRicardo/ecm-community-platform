# ECM: PV Integration with School Roof and Site Planning

## Summary
Preserve and use roof/site area for photovoltaic generation so the school can realistically close the remaining annual energy gap after efficiency measures.

## Estimated Savings
- **AEDG basis**: required final-leg strategy for most on-site zero-energy schools.
- **Typical effect**: site-specific offset of purchased electricity.
- **End-Uses Affected**: grid electricity purchased by the school.

## Basis / References
- The zero-energy K-12 guide frames renewable energy as essential to balancing annual consumption.
- It separates design strategy from implementation strategy, which is critical for practical delivery.

## Assumptions
Requires that efficiency measures are already strong enough for available roof/site area to matter.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones; yield varies with solar resource and shading.

## Interaction Notes
Competes with rooftop equipment, daylighting apertures, and maintenance access.

## Implementation Essentials
- Protect PV zones early in roof planning.
- Coordinate structure, electrical rooms, conduit routes, and future expansion.
- Model annual production against school load profile.
- Consider how the PV system can also serve educational visibility goals.

## Risks / Constraints
- Mechanical clutter and late design changes can destroy useful PV area.
- Interconnection and procurement logistics can delay implementation.

## KPIs
- Annual PV generation
- PV capacity installed vs potential
- Self-consumption ratio
- Lost roof area to non-PV conflicts

## M&V Plan
- Direct generation metering and inverter trend review.
- Compare actual output against modeled annual yield.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Strategic capex measure; usually necessary for full on-site zero energy.


## Templates / Reuse
- ECM screening worksheet
- Climate-zone applicability note
- KPI / M&V checklist
