# ECM: Energy Dashboard and Student/Staff Engagement Program

## Summary
Use visible dashboards and occupant engagement so staff and students reinforce rather than undermine zero-energy operation.

## Estimated Savings
- **Typical effect**: diffuse but important persistence benefit across lighting, plug loads, and HVAC behavior.
- **End-Uses Affected**: whole-building operations indirectly.

## Basis / References
- The zero-energy K-12 guide explicitly discusses school-community engagement and dashboard-based learning.
- The building is treated as both infrastructure and teaching tool.

## Assumptions
Works only if data are understandable and used repeatedly, not installed as static display décor.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Supports plug-load control, schedule adherence, and override management.

## Implementation Essentials
- Display a small set of understandable KPIs, not a cluttered dashboard.
- Tie energy information into curriculum, staff orientation, and operations meetings.
- Use dashboards to identify anomalies and reinforce behavior.
- Keep FM ownership clear.

## Risks / Constraints
- Dashboards without programmatic follow-through have little persistence value.
- Poorly explained data can confuse rather than engage users.

## KPIs
- After-hours load
- Plug-load intensity
- Override counts
- Dashboard usage/engagement metrics

## M&V Plan
- Track after-hours energy and policy adherence before/after engagement rollout.
- Correlate dashboard use with identified operational corrections.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low to moderate; strongest as persistence support for other ECMs.
