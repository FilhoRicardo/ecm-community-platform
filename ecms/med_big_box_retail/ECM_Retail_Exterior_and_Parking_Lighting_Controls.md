# ECM: Exterior and Parking Lighting Reduction with Zoning and Late-Night Setback

## Summary
Reduce parking-lot and exterior lighting power and runtime while maintaining safety, wayfinding, and security camera performance.

## Estimated Savings
- **Typical effect**: strong site-lighting savings where parking fields and façade lighting operate at full output all night.
- **End-Uses Affected**: exterior lighting electricity.

## Basis / References
- The retail AEDG includes exterior-lighting implementation guidance and parking-lot-specific discussion.
- Community impact and retail safety are both recognized.

## Assumptions
Best where parking and façade lighting are broadly controlled rather than zoned by actual need.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Works with LED fixture upgrades, astronomical control, and security operational review.

## Implementation Essentials
- Reduce installed power where overlighting exists.
- Zone parking, perimeter, loading, and façade lighting separately.
- Use photocells plus schedules or dimming for late-night periods.
- Confirm CCTV and security requirements before final setpoints.

## Risks / Constraints
- Security stakeholders may resist without objective criteria.
- Poor zoning can force full-site operation for one active area.

## KPIs
- Exterior lighting kWh
- Late-night runtime at full output
- Lighting level compliance in critical areas
- Security incident / complaint trend

## M&V Plan
- Compare site-lighting runtime and energy pre/post.
- Verify dimming/schedule compliance through controller logs.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Often favorable, particularly when runtime reduction is the dominant savings source.
