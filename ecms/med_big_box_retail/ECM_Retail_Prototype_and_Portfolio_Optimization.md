# ECM: Retail Prototype Standardization and Continuous Improvement

## Summary
Use an energy-informed retail prototype and portfolio feedback loop so efficient measures are repeatable across climates without losing brand consistency.

## Estimated Savings
- **AEDG basis**: strategic portfolio measure rather than a single end-use retrofit.
- **Typical effect**: improves repeatability, speeds adoption of proven ECMs, and reduces drift between design intent and rollout.
- **End-Uses Affected**: whole-building performance indirectly.

## Basis / References
- The medium/big-box retail AEDG emphasizes integrated design, prototype refinement, benchmarking, and portfolio learning.
- Retail chains gain value by applying lessons repeatedly rather than redesigning from scratch.

## Assumptions
Most useful for portfolios or repeat-format retailers rather than one-off stores.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Supports HVAC standardization, lighting packages, exterior lighting controls, and prototype-specific envelope/climate variants.

## Implementation Essentials
- Create a baseline prototype with climate-zone variants.
- Benchmark operating stores and feed lessons into the next revision.
- Standardize preferred lighting/HVAC/control packages where performance is proven.
- Distinguish which elements are fixed by brand and which can vary by climate.

## Risks / Constraints
- Brand standards can freeze inefficient details if not reviewed.
- Portfolio teams may standardize first-cost decisions rather than best practice.

## KPIs
- EUI by prototype generation
- ECM adoption rate across stores
- Measured vs design performance variance
- Utility cost intensity by climate

## M&V Plan
- Use store benchmarking and normalized portfolio comparisons.
- Track prototype revisions against actual store outcomes.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low analytical/process cost with high portfolio leverage.


## Templates / Reuse
- ECM screening worksheet
- Climate-zone applicability note
- KPI / M&V checklist
