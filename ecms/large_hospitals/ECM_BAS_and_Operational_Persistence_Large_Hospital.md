# ECM: BAS Governance, Trend Review, and Hospital Operational Persistence

## Summary
Use BAS governance and trend review to keep high-complexity hospital sequences functioning as intended rather than slowly drifting into constant overrides.

## Estimated Savings
- **Typical effect**: protects multiple major ECMs rather than acting as a narrow hardware upgrade.
- **End-Uses Affected**: whole-building controls persistence.

## Basis / References
- The large-hospital guide emphasizes controls, commissioning, O&M, and M&V as core to delivering the target savings.
- Hospital systems are too complex for one-time setup alone.

## Assumptions
Requires named ownership of trend review, alarm rationalization, and override management.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Supports ventilation, reheat, lighting, plant, and pressure-control ECMs across the facility.

## Implementation Essentials
- Trend a concise set of key control points by department and system type.
- Investigate repeated overrides and persistent alarms rather than normalizing them.
- Recommission after space-use changes and clinical moves.
- Train operations staff on intended operating envelopes.

## Risks / Constraints
- BAS data may exist but go unused.
- Clinical workarounds can silently eliminate savings for months or years.

## KPIs
- Override counts
- Persistent alarm count
- Monthly EUI by department where possible
- Sequence-of-operations compliance checks

## M&V Plan
- Whole-building Option C with targeted subsystem trend review.
- Use seasonal exception reporting for persistence.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low soft-cost measure with high lifecycle value.


## Templates / Reuse
- ECM screening worksheet
- Climate-zone applicability note
- KPI / M&V checklist
