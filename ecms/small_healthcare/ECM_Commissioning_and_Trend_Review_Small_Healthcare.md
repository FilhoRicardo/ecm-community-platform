# ECM: Small Healthcare Commissioning and Trend-Based Persistence

## Summary
Use commissioning, trend review, and early operational correction to preserve the value of ventilation, lighting, and DHW ECMs in healthcare settings.

## Estimated Savings
- **Typical effect**: protects multiple ECMs rather than acting as a stand-alone hardware measure.
- **End-Uses Affected**: whole-building operational performance.

## Basis / References
- The healthcare AEDG emphasizes QA, commissioning, monitoring, and training as essential to achieving the package savings target.
- This is particularly important where controls and ventilation complexity are high.

## Assumptions
Requires active owner/operator involvement and usable BAS trend data.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Supports every major measure, especially DOAS, reheat reduction, and clinical lighting controls.

## Implementation Essentials
- Commission airflow, pressure, humidity, scenes, and plant control sequences.
- Trend key points through at least the first representative seasonal cycle.
- Investigate repeated overrides and comfort workarounds.
- Train FM staff on intended operating bands.

## Risks / Constraints
- Staff turnover and clinical workarounds can degrade persistence quickly.
- BAS trend data may be available but not reviewed.

## KPIs
- Monthly EUI
- Reheat intensity
- Humidity excursions
- Override and alarm closure rates

## M&V Plan
- Whole-building Option C supported by subsystem trends.
- Use issue-log closure and seasonal review as persistence checks.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low soft-cost measure with strong lifecycle value.


## Templates / Reuse
- ECM screening worksheet
