# ECM: Gymnasium Toplighting with Daylight Controls

## Summary
Use toplighting in gymnasiums so daytime lighting energy drops while sports visibility and assembly use remain acceptable.

## Estimated Savings
- **Typical effect**: strong daytime lighting reduction in large-volume spaces.
- **End-Uses Affected**: gym lighting and some secondary cooling.

## Basis / References
- The 50% K-12 guide includes dedicated gym-toplighting strategies.
- These spaces are especially suitable because they are deep-plan and often daytime occupied.

## Assumptions
Best where school schedules align with daylight hours and roof detailing is managed carefully.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones with appropriate thermal control.

## Interaction Notes
Requires daylight controls and good glare management.

## Implementation Essentials
- Use diffuse toplighting rather than direct beam openings.
- Keep sports and assembly viewing conditions in mind.
- Pair with dimming or stepped controls.
- Coordinate maintenance and roof access early.

## Risks / Constraints
- Poor diffusion can create bright patches and glare.
- Owners may resist if leakage or maintenance history is poor.

## KPIs
- Gym lighting kWh
- Daytime full-output runtime
- User glare complaints
- Maintenance incidents related to toplighting

## M&V Plan
- Compare daytime lighting energy against baseline or similar non-toplit spaces.
- Review daylight-control operation and override behavior.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Moderate to high; strongest in large-volume school spaces.
