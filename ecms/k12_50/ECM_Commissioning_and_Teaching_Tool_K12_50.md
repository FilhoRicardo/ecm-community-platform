# ECM: School Commissioning with Teaching-Tool and Staff Training Integration

## Summary
Commission the school and train staff/teachers so the building performs as designed and can also be used as a visible educational asset.

## Estimated Savings
- **Typical effect**: persistence and cultural support for multiple ECMs rather than a narrow hardware measure.
- **End-Uses Affected**: whole-building operations indirectly.

## Basis / References
- The 50% K-12 guide emphasizes commissioning, M&V, O&M training, and the idea of the building as a teaching tool.
- Teacher and staff behavior influence actual performance.

## Assumptions
Works best when dashboard data and operations training are maintained beyond turnover.

## Climate Zone Relevance
All climate zones.

## Interaction Notes
Supports controls persistence, plug-load discipline, and after-hours schedule compliance.

## Implementation Essentials
- Commission envelope, lighting, kitchens, and HVAC with trend verification.
- Train FM staff, administrators, and teachers on how the building is supposed to operate.
- Use dashboards or visible metrics to reinforce expected behavior.
- Revisit settings after the first seasonal cycle.

## Risks / Constraints
- Turnover-only training is rarely enough.
- Dashboards without ownership have weak persistence value.

## KPIs
- Monthly EUI
- Override counts
- After-hours load
- Training completion / engagement indicators

## M&V Plan
- Whole-building Option C with targeted system trend review.
- Track performance drift through the first year and after academic calendar changes.

## Costs & Payback (indicative)
- Low soft-cost measure with strong persistence value.


## Templates / Reuse
- ECM screening worksheet
- Climate-zone applicability note
- KPI / M&V checklist
