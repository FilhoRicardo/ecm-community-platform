# ECM: BMS Setpoint & Schedule Optimisation (Seasonal)

## Summary

**What:** Reduce heating setpoints from 23°C to 21°C working / 16°C setback; tighten deadbands; align HVAC run schedules with actual occupancy (not default 7am–7pm 5-day). Each 1°C reduction saves ~6–8% heating energy.

**Why:** Appears at 7 of 8 properties. Schedule overruns of 1–2 hrs/day add 10–15% excess HVAC runtime. Basic operational measure not being done systematically anywhere.

## Estimated Savings
- 12–20% heating energy
- Building-specific

## Basis / References
- Portfolio basis: 7/8 European commercial properties
- CIBSE Guide F: Energy Efficiency

## Assumptions
- Building-specific survey required before implementation
- Energy audit recommended to quantify baseline and savings

## Implementation Essentials
1. 1) Export current BAS schedules and setpoints. 2) Reduce heating setpoint 2°C. 3) Tighten deadband to ±1°C. 4) Align AHU start/stop to actual occupancy — typically 6am–6pm weekdays. 5) Document and formalize into seasonal procedure. 6) Communicate changes to tenants in advance.

## Risks / Constraints
- HIGH PRIORITY — appears at 7/8 buildings without evidence of systematic implementation. No cost, highest ROI action.

## KPIs
- Heating energy (kWh/HDD)
- HVAC runtime hours/day
- Tenant comfort complaints
- Setpoint compliance (%)

- Post-installation: compare same period following year (weather-normalised)
- IPMVP Option C: Whole-building metering with regression analysis
